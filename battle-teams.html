How the team builder thinks

The leaders are the top-scoring Pokémon in the live PvPoke overall rankings for the league you pick. When you choose a leader, the app builds an ABB-style team (one lead + two coverage picks) using this logic:

  1. It reads your leader's type weaknesses and its top counters (the Pokémon that beat it, straight from PvPoke sim data).
  2. It scans the top ~80 meta Pokémon and scores each on: resisting your leader's weaknesses, beating your leader's counters (per PvPoke matchup data), and not sharing weaknesses with the rest of the team.
  3. It greedily picks teammate A, then re-checks the trio's combined weaknesses and picks teammate B to patch the remaining holes.

Movesets are PvPoke's recommended set. Elite TM means the move only comes from an Elite Charged/Fast TM (GO Battle League rewards, special events, or paid research) — you can't get it from a normal TM. Legacy means it was tied to a past event/Community Day and generally needs an Elite TM today. Max CP is the highest CP that stays legal under the league cap with perfect 15/15/15 IVs, plus the level it takes. For deeper matchup sims and shield scenarios, this app links you straight into PvPoke.
